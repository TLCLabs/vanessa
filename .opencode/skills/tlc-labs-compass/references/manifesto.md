# TLC Labs

## Our Vision

*"To fundamentally improve the lives of people by creating a compassionate, employee-first business model, while building reliable open-source appliances that help eliminate e-waste"* - TLC Labs

## Core Identity: T.L.C.

- Transparency - To build trust you must have full transparency, we will be fully transparent with our employees and people.
- Longevity - We are building an ever-lasting institution that produces devices and relationships designed to stand the test of time.
- Community - We prioritize our people and foster a community where our employees are loved.

## Operational Hierarchy

- **First - Our Employees:** We believe that happy employees will naturally keep our people happy, which in turn creates a prosperous company.
- **Second - People:** We are dedicated to providing unbelievable hospitality alongside high-quality products.
- **Third - Income & Longevity:** We must generate income to stay afloat, operating with a mindset of surviving for "5 more years, every year".

## What We Do & How We Earn

- **Product Focus:** We develop competitive mid-range to high-range open-source appliances.
- **Manufacturing Method:** Our devices are built using a combination of 3D-printed parts and standard off-the-shelf hardware and electronics.
- **Revenue Streams:** We generate income strictly by selling pre-assembled devices, self-assembly kits, and standalone hardware components.
- **Business Structure:** We operate as a registered Sole Proprietor in Saskatchewan but are built upon a Social Enterprise (B-Corp) model.

## Structural Empathy (How We Work)

- **10:1 Pay Ratio:** Company leaders and the CEO will never earn more than ten times the salary of our lowest-paid employee.
- **32-Hour Work Week:** We mandate a 4-day work week, so employees receive 40-hour pay for 32 hours of work, allowing them more time with their families.
- **Mandatory Rest:** We provide competitive benefits, including 6 weeks of vacation, 4 of which are mandatory to ensure a rested and sturdy mind.

## Our Promise to People

- **Vocabulary Shift:** We do not use the word "Customers"; we only serve, build for, and help "People".
- **Absolute Ownership:** We embrace true transparency, open-source principles, and the absolute right-to-repair, so people truly own what they buy.
- **No Subscriptions:** We refuse to drain the wallets of our people and guarantee absolutely no subscriptions.
- **Environmental Impact:** We are committed to reducing e-waste and will explore circular-economy initiatives like plastic recycling discounts.
