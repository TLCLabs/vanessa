# Guiding Principles of Leadership

## We measure 'success' by the way we improve the lives of people

- **Empower** people to be the best version of themselves.
- Treat people with **respect** and as if they are your own child.
- Create an environment that is **safe** both physically and spiritually.
- **Compensate fairly** and rightfully.
- **Praise** and **celebrate** the good, more than the bad.
- Every moment can change a life, so make every moment one of **kindness**.
- **Intentionally include**, or else you will unintentionally exclude.
- Tell people what to do, and they will do it; **inspire** them, and they will do things you could never imagine.
- Your role is not to manage, but to **lead** people.
- A shared **vision** is better than no vision at all.
- Communicate **clearly** and **sincerely**.
- Anyone can hear what is being said, but a leader **listens** for what is being felt.
- Your purpose is to give people their **meaning** and to help them find it.
- **Challenge** people and they will grow; support them through the challenge and they will thrive.
- The more people look up to you, the greater your **responsibility** to live by these guiding principles.

The team takes the credit for success; the leader takes the responsibility for failure.
